# Critter Cove 🐣🌸

A cosy little farm game you play in your browser. Raise happy animals, grow crops,
adopt wild critters, keep bees, decorate your house and make the prettiest farm on the island.

**▶ Play:** open `index.html`, or visit the GitHub Pages link for this repo.

## How to play
- **Move:** WASD / arrow keys, or click where you want to go
- **Pat animals:** click them. Fill the hay feeder and water trough every day
- **Farm:** click your field to plant, click seedlings to water, click ripe crops to harvest
- **Earn coins:** sell eggs, milk, wool, crops, fish, flowers, logs, stone and honey at the market stall
- **Adopt wild critters:** pat a frog, mouse, swan or capybara, then give it a critter snack
- **Build mode:** press **B** to place, move, pick up or remove things. **Ctrl+Z** undoes
- **Your house:** click it to go inside, decorate, and sleep in your bed after 6pm
- Three save plots; your farm saves by itself

## Putting it on GitHub Pages
1. Create a new repository and upload `index.html` and this `README.md`.
2. In the repo go to **Settings → Pages**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
3. After a minute your game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
